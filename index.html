About Me

A developer from Thakurgaon, Bangladesh, turning ideas into working software.

I am a Computer Science student at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), currently pursuing my BSc in Computer Science & Engineering. Before that, I completed a four-year Diploma in Engineering in Computer Science & Technology from Thakurgaon Government Polytechnic Institute, graduating with a CGPA of 3.90 out of 4.00.

I work as a freelance developer with HTML, CSS and Python. I enjoy building websites that look good on every screen and desktop applications that solve real, everyday problems — from business calculators to complete management systems with databases.

I take on project-based freelance work and give every project focused, honest effort — 2 to 3 hours a day, with clear communication from start to finish.
